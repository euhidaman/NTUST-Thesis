HOW LEARNING HAPPENS:
---------------------

Stage 2 Training combines TWO learning signals:

1. Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) Loss - 70% weight
   - Cross-entropy on multimodal instruction data
   - Teaches model to follow instructions
   - Ground truth: human-annotated responses

2. Hidden State Distillation - 30% weight
   - Aligns student's internal representations with teacher
   - Teacher: SmolVLM2-2.2B (2.2B params, SOTA VLM)
   - Student: EmberVLM (135M params)
   - Loss: MSE between projected hidden states
   - Transfers knowledge WITHOUT vocab matching

Total Loss = 0.7 * SFT_Loss + 0.3 * Distillation_Loss
